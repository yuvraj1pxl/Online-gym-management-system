BMI — Body Mass Index

BMI is a quick screening tool that estimates body fatness using your height and weight ratio. It helps identify if you're in a healthy weight range.

Underweight
Below 18.5
Normal
18.5 – 24.9
Overweight
25 – 29.9
Obese
30 and above

BMR — Basal Metabolic Rate

BMR is the number of calories your body burns at complete rest. Knowing your BMR helps you calculate your total daily energy expenditure (TDEE) and set accurate caloric targets for fat loss or muscle gain.
